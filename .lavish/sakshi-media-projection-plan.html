The experience from capture to evidence

The flow shows the recommended review-first option. Retention and recovery policy still need your decision. Burst capture follows manual scrolling; it does not control other apps.

What exists, and what must be built

Existing prototype

  • ImageReader / VirtualDisplay frames and PNG encoding
  • Single-use token wrapper and frame sampling
  • OCR/layout heuristics and direct vault import
  • Unit-test source, currently outside the app build

Required integration

  • Real OS consent launcher and foreground service
  • Visible controls, cancellation, rotation and limits
  • Encrypted staging while the vault is locked
  • Review, idempotent save, provenance and device tests

Correct two prototype claims before shipping

A black frame cannot prove FLAG_SECURE, View Once, or Private Space. Chat layout cannot establish sender identity, direction, or message time. Keep these unknown or user-reviewed.

Implementation in verifiable slices

  1. M0 · Freeze decisions and contracts. Validate OS consent, service, app-window capture and stop on synthetic device screens.
  2. M1 · One screenshot end to end. Consent → capture → encrypted draft → unlock/review → encrypted case evidence with matching hash.
  3. M2 · Bounded bursts. Sampling, backpressure, native geometry, rotation, exact duplicate grouping and honest capture gaps.
  4. M3 · Local interpretation. Offline OCR and source-linked suggestions, unknown attribution, overlap-aware patterns and reviewed reports.
  5. M4 · Optional screen video. Encoded encrypted segments, bounded recording, playback, frame selection and explicit export.
  6. M5 · Optional internal audio. Separate consent, permitted app playback only, synchronized tracks and optional offline STT.
  7. M6 · Device acceptance. Lifecycle, privacy, faults, limits, upgrade/rollback and existing collection regressions.

Decisions still open

D-001 · Evidence modes

Video/audio need their own storage, playback, permission and resource acceptance gates.

D-002 · Retention

Automatic retention requires a new constrained key/ingress design. It cannot keep the vault unlocked during capture.

D-003 · Proposed temporary-key policy

An in-memory session key encrypts temporary drafts. Unsaved drafts are lost on process death and discarded on lock or revocation. Stopped drafts expire after a proposed five-minute review window. Durable recovery needs a separately approved security design.

D-004 · Visible controls

Proposed: require usable foreground notification controls before starting. Android itself does not require notification permission to start a foreground service. This policy needs product approval.

D-005 · Proposed screenshot bounds

120 seconds/session · 20 retained frames · 1 sampled frame/second · 4 megapixels/frame · 16 MiB PNG/frame · 128 MiB encrypted drafts. Proposed engineering budgets, not measured performance or approved requirements.

What acceptance must prove

Privacy and evidence

Fresh consent, no plaintext files, vault stays locked, authenticated review, byte/hash parity, original preservation, no hidden exports, source-linked suggestions and unknown attribution.

Actual Android behavior

Denial, stop, lock, OS revocation, process death, startup failures, window resize, low space, repeat saves and measured bounds. Emulator/JVM checks supplement real-device capture evidence.

MediaProjection records permitted visible output. Share/Import remains the route for original attachments. Protected content is respected. Internal audio depends on the source app's policies and eligible playback.

Sakshi / State of the art / 02 October 2026

The ingredients exist.
The integration must be proven.

Existing systems already cover abuse journals, local message AI, temporal detection, encrypted vaults, provenance and reports. Sakshi's potential differentiation is an evaluated, survivor-controlled Android workflow joining those capabilities without cloud AI in its core.

Overlaps that matter most

AI + abuse documentation is established

Aimee Says documents pattern analysis across uploaded materials. A founder interview describes generated events, timelines, approval/editing and evidence attachments. Android-local execution and forensic byte lineage remain unestablished here.

Local + temporal safety AI is prior art

Agent Hita describes Android-local conversation analysis and a temporal risk engine. It deliberately does not preserve raw messages. Public code is source available, not open source; effectiveness was not independently validated.

Offline evidence protection is established

Tella combines offline capture/import, encrypted local files, verification hashes/metadata and selective sharing. Proofmode covers signatures and provenance; signing is not encryption.

AI + preserved records + reporting exists

OurFamilyWizard combines AI-assisted communication and permanent hosted records. TalkingParents signs exported PDFs. Axon Evidence integrates AI, custody, auditability and disclosure.

Lifecycle matters. DocuSAFE ceased download availability in October 2023. Perspective API officially ends on December 31, 2026. Historical prior art remains relevant even when a service is discontinued.

C-D. Established capabilities and less-evidenced gaps

Common within relevant categories

  • Manual incident journals and multimedia attachments.
  • Chronological logs, labels, impact ratings and reports.
  • User-selected sharing with helpers/legal professionals.
  • PINs, quick exit, discreet UI and safety warnings.
  • AI toxicity/tone scores and human moderation.
  • Own-platform permanent messages, calls and transcripts.
  • Hashes, signatures, provenance and custody auditing.
  • Multilingual interfaces and selected classifiers.
  • Local notification history and local model inference.

Uncommon or poorly evidenced end-to-end

  • Network-denied acquisition-to-report local AI workflow.
  • Calibrated temporal findings on partial private evidence.
  • Exact original-to-OCR/STT-to-claim source lineage.
  • Validated Indic, Romanized and code-mixed performance.
  • Capture-completeness-aware patterns and deduplication.
  • Selective offline exports with an independent verifier.
  • Corrections propagated into patterns without rewriting evidence.
  • Survivor-safety evaluation and real-phone energy benchmarks.

These are evidence gaps, not proof that no other system has them.

E. Potentially differentiated combinations

  1. Supported Android intake + encrypted originals + offline multimodal AI + reviewed temporal reports. Ingredients exist across notification tools, Tella, local inference apps and Aimee/research.
  2. Local temporal reasoning + opt-in evidence retention + correction-aware patterns. Distinguish from transient raw-message processing and unsupported machine judgments.
  3. Indic/code-mixed validation + acquisition-realistic noise + uncertainty-aware reporting. Language claims need measured domain performance.
  4. Cryptographic derivative lineage + selective redaction + portable offline verifier. Build on existing signing/custody formats, not proprietary unverifiable claims.
  5. Survivor-centered local custody + explicit recovery/transfer + safe disclosure controls. Address both device-search risk and evidence-loss risk.

Candidate product combinations, not patentable-invention claims. Each needs a precise feature definition and empirical verification.

F. Do not claim as novel

Do not claim “first abuse documentation app,” “first AI-generated evidence timeline,” “first Android-local harassment AI,” “first temporal bullying detection,” or “first AI + evidence + reporting system.” Do not describe encryption, PIN/decoy UI, OCR/STT, SHA-256, signatures, quantization, hybrid routing or abstention as newly invented.

Do not equate public code with an open-source license, privacy marketing with on-device inference, a signature with truth, or notification/accessibility access with complete message access.

Safer positioning: Sakshi is being designed as an Android-first, local-default workflow for user-controlled evidence, uncertainty-aware pattern suggestions and traceable reports. Its claims should be tied to published tests.

G. Ten grounded differentiation opportunities

Every opportunity in the full report includes prior art, implementation details, tests and limits.

1. Enforceable evidence-linked findings

Require original IDs/hashes and exact text/image/audio references. Schema validation rejects fabricated references. Measure unsupported-claim rate; valid citations alone do not prove correct interpretation.

2. Capture-completeness-aware temporal detection

Track permissions, listener state, coverage and timestamp origin. Deduplicate notification updates and imports. Replay missing/grouped/out-of-order evidence and measure false escalation alerts.

3. Original-to-derivative provenance graph

Preserve immutable originals; version OCR, STT, labels, edits and redactions separately. Tamper tests must catch mutations in content, lineage edges and reports. Imported forged screenshots remain a distinct authenticity problem.

4. Public Indic/code-mixed robustness evaluation

Evaluate English, Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi, Hinglish and Romanized/code-mixed text with conversation/person/source-safe splits. Publish per-language errors, calibration and OCR/STT sensitivity.

5. Measured local inference cascade

Cheap filter, compact classifier, optional small local LLM, deterministic temporal aggregate. Benchmark p95 latency, RAM, battery and thermal behavior against always-LLM and simple baselines on modest phones.

6. Corrections that propagate without rewriting evidence

Keep machine suggestions, user decisions and approved labels versioned. Test that edits/merges/rejections update all aggregate patterns and subsequent reports while original hashes remain fixed.

7. Portable selective export and independent verifier

User-reviewed reports plus machine-readable manifests and chosen originals/derivatives. Verify offline on a fresh machine; test PDF layers, filenames, thumbnails, metadata and OCR text for redaction leakage.

8. Published threat model and recovery drills

Document locked/unlocked, shared-account, root and coercive-access limits. Audit caches, logs, backups and crash reports. Test explicit encrypted transfer and lost-key recovery; camouflage is not invisibility.

9. Bounded, survivor-safe assistance

Keep reports factual and resources curated. No autonomous confrontation advice, legal conclusions or recipient alerts. Evaluate unsafe/missing guidance with domain experts separately from classifier accuracy.

10. Reproducible longitudinal benchmarks

Define outcomes for repetition, frequency and transitions. Report pattern precision/recall, false alerts per observed week, delay, calibration and abstention. Do not market a generic uncalibrated “danger probability.”

Acquisition and security boundaries

  • NotificationListenerService observes exposed notifications, not private databases. Android 15 additionally redacts detected OTP content for untrusted listeners.
  • Prefer user shares, SAF/Photo Picker and scoped notifications; preserve originals separately from analysis.
  • SMS permissions and accessibility distribution constraints require actual device/policy validation.
  • No source establishes legitimate extraction of protected WhatsApp View Once payloads. An observed notification or user description is a different evidence type.
  • No INTERNET permission limits direct app networking, not all IPC, exports, backups or compromised-device access.
  • 2026 expert evaluation of TFA assistants documents inaccurate/incomplete guidance and missing safety warnings under a defined study protocol. Fluent or empathic AI output is not sufficient safety validation.

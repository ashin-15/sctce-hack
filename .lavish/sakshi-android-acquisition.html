Do not promise all chats.

Android notification access is an official interface to what an app publishes. It is not permission to read that app's database, recover deleted content, or extract protected View Once media.

Research, not device certification or legal clearance. Exact notification extras, sender mapping, URI grants and OEM delivery still need real-phone tests. Legal authorization, Android permissions, provider terms and Play approval are separate gates.

Full engineering specification: research/android-evidence-acquisition-specification.md. It includes 90 capability-app rows, nine mechanism assessments, Android-version gates, provenance fields, consent flows, 12 acceptance requirements and a source register with inspection limitations.

Three engineering decisions

Preferred / A + B

User-selected imports

Sharesheet, SAF, Photo Picker. Preserve selected text, files, screenshots, ordinary media and public exports. No broad storage permission needed for properly granted items.

High feasibility after a readable copy. Selection does not prove completeness, sender identity or source authenticity. Android sources

Opt-in / A + C

Notification observations

Read-only NLS collector. Package/profile, posting time, exposed extras, sender hints and media labels. Structured MessagingStyle first, conservative generic fallback.

No source notification means no capture. Reconnect retrieves outstanding notifications, not a missed-message journal. NLS contract

Unsupported / D or E

Protected and private data

No private database or View Once extraction. No automatic recovery of uncaptured deleted messages. No accessibility or screen-capture fallback to defeat protection.

Offer a permitted import or a clearly labeled user statement. Unknown content stays unknown. Sandbox contract

Mechanisms: data exposed versus authority granted

NotificationListenerService - appropriate with explicit opt-in

Receives posted/updated/removed/ranked notifications. Snapshot exposed text, package/profile, time, group and optional structured message data. Source apps choose content; no private history or guaranteed full media.

Wait for listener connection. Reconnect snapshots only outstanding records. Removal is not chat deletion. Keep callbacks short; encrypted bounded persistence before deferred AI. Silent notifications may be relevant; DND is not necessarily no notification. Primary source

Sharesheet, SAF and document/file providers - preferred

Per-item text/URI grants and user selection. Copy the readable received artifact after preview/Save, before transient grants expire. Persist SAF access only if the result actually permits it. Providers may be remote, revoke access or return a transcoded representation.

A provider URI is not permission to enumerate its owner's private files. Android 11 restricts Android/data and Android/obb selection; individual ordinary Downloads files remain selectable. Photo Picker selects images/video, not audio. Sharing / SAF

Scoped storage and sandbox - no private cross-app read

Shared allowed media can be selected. Broad storage permissions do not reveal messenger databases or private caches. Android/media shared storage is distinct from Android/data app-specific storage. All-files access still excludes other apps' app-specific data and is not justified for this MVP.

Own encrypted vault is required. Exclude evidence and derivatives from cloud/D2D/migration by default; allowBackup=false alone is not a universal OEM D2D guarantee. No silent backup-key extraction. Sandbox / Backup

AccessibilityService - not the baseline collector

Can expose accessible visible UI text/nodes that an app publishes, not a stable full-chat export. Virtualized/offscreen/sensitive nodes can be missing. Modern Android restricts non-tool access to sensitive views; screenshot controls and node restrictions are separate.

Non-disability usage is not categorically forbidden, but needs declaration, approval and dedicated disclosure/consent. Sakshi must not falsely declare isAccessibilityTool=true. No broad silent scraping or autonomous chat navigation. Policy / Android security

MediaProjection - optional short user-started sessions

Capturable visible pixels only, not original files, offscreen history or protected screens. Android 14 hardens session consent/tokens and FGS requirements; Android 15 QPR1 exposes a prominent chip and stops at screen lock. Handle onStop and release resources.

Internal audio requires RECORD_AUDIO, approved projection, same profile, permitted usage and source capture policy. Voice communication is not generally capturable; voice notes are conditional. Never use capture to defeat View Once protection. Projection / Playback capture

SMS/MMS - separate policy and permission gate

Authorized Telephony surfaces provide body/address/time/retained SMS history; MMS parts are separate and RCS is not automatically included. Role/installer authority, runtime grant and Play eligibility are independent requirements.

Play policy forbids alternative API derivation as a workaround. Do not use notification/accessibility access to evade SMS restrictions. The physical-safety exception lists SEND_SMS, not unrestricted reading. Keep automatic SMS capture disabled until eligibility is established. Full policy

The app-controlled limits that change the plan

WhatsApp export can be blocked

Ordinary export supplies text and recent media. Advanced Chat Privacy can prevent export and automatic gallery saving. View Once is not an original-media ingestion route.

Exact Android notification schema and View Once labels need device tests. Sources

Meta ephemeral capture is protected

Instagram/Messenger View Once and Allow Replay screenshot/recording prevention contradicts older “capture works but notifies” guidance. Archives are selected, retained-data snapshots, not deleted-content recovery.

Messenger E2EE download is documented on computer. Primary announcement

Telegram is the legitimate API exception

TDLib can be an independently authorized online client for permitted cloud history. It cannot read an existing official client's device-specific secret chat. Respect content protection, including private chats.

Desktop export import is the simpler MVP path. Sources

Signal history support is not settled

Official repository plaintext-export underpinnings exist, but the inspected commit includes internal playground UI. That is not proof of stable public Android availability. Ordinary media save/import is documented.

Do not say “never exportable” or “shipping everywhere.” Official commit

Current Android changes visibility

Android 15 OTP redaction, sensitive screenshare protection and private-space stoppage; Android 16 sensitive accessibility controls; Android 17 SMS OTP availability delays.

Android 17's unexplained restricted-message summary entry is not proof that all E2EE previews are blocked. Sources

Two independent delivery failures

The source messaging app may not post a notification. Sakshi's listener may be disconnected. OEM app sleep and battery policy affect both; connected does not mean complete.

Test actual model/build/client, not just “Android support.” Documented source-app risks
